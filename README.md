# Modern Watchface

Modern is a configurable analog watchface for the Pebble Time 2 (`emery`). It combines a custom-rendered dial and hands with a modular information display. The watch app is written in C; the phone-side JavaScript app supplies settings, weather and astronomy data, location handling, background-image transfer, presets, and scheduled styles.

## Highlights

- **Custom analog dial:** independently configurable hour, minute, and optional second hands, including multiple hand shapes, dimensions, offsets, colors, outlines, translucency, hollow centres, shadows, and a configurable centre cap.
- **Dial indices:** choose bitmap marker designs or procedural marker presets. Configure hour and second rings, marker text, Roman numerals, and bitmap blending options.
- **Information features:** place readouts in the available corner and edge positions. Content includes date and time, time zones, battery and Bluetooth status, Pebble Quiet Time, health metrics, weather, air quality, and astronomical information. Individual feature slots support color and shake-only variants; custom primitive compositions are available for supported positions.
- **Weather-aware appearance:** weather icons, current conditions, forecast values, optional weather-driven backgrounds, and a weather-responsive border. Weather sources include the Pebble app/plugin and Open-Meteo, with optional OpenWeatherMap data.
- **Astronomy:** moon phase, sunrise and sunset, visible planets and rise times, meteor showers, Saturn ring angle, ISS passes, and aurora activity. Location is supplied automatically by phone GPS or entered manually.
- **Personal styling:** day and optional night color schemes, color presets, custom and bundled backgrounds, weather backgrounds, and saved style presets.
- **Animation and power controls:** startup and shake animations, optional feature reveal on shake, battery-saving behavior, configurable data refresh, hourly vibration reminders, and Bluetooth-loss vibration.
- **Phone configuration:** live watchface preview, example styles, editable hand and marker controls, font and icon selectors, style import/export, up to six saved presets, and optional day/time schedules for presets.

## Settings guide

The settings page is bundled with the app and is generated by the phone-side JavaScript. Its main sections are:

### Example styles

Preview ready-made designs. Applying an example replaces the related Style, Colors, and Features settings. It is a starting point that can be further customized.

### Style

- **Hands:** select a hand design and edit hour, minute, and second hands separately. Available properties include width, length, rear and middle offsets, secondary width, color, outline, translucency, shadow, and hollow styling. The centre circle has its own radius and color.
- **Indices:** select the hour/second marker design, including built-in bitmap designs and procedural presets. Depending on the design, additional options control transparency, inversion, custom ring geometry, and marker text. Marker text supports font, offset, hour/second masks, and Roman numerals.
- **Background:** choose a solid/custom background, a bundled image, or a weather-driven background style. Personal images can be imported from the phone and transferred to the watch.
  - **PLUGIN** (fourth background style) shows a picture supplied by a Pebble plugin that offers an `image` property. Pick the source, then dithering (none, Floyd–Steinberg, Atkinson, ordered 4x4/8x8), color processing (normal, vivid, black & white, sepia) and screen filling (fill, show full image, 75 %, 50 %, original size centered). While the picture is transferred the watch shows the same progress bar as for personal images. **Cache picture on the watch** stores it in the watch's persistent storage (instead of RAM only) so a watchface restart does not trigger another full transfer; the phone only re-sends when the picture or the option changed. The phone asks the plugin for a picture exactly the size of the watch's screen (emery 200x228, gabbro 260x260, black-and-white watches 144x168). **Day/night colors from picture brightness** switches the watch to the day colors for a bright picture and to the night colors for a dark one (with hysteresis, independent of sunrise/sunset): set a white background in the day colors and a dark background in the night colors. The phone does all of the processing (`bg-image/plugin-image.js`) and sends the finished 64-color picture over the same transfer as personal images, into a RAM-only slot on the watch: the watch knows nothing about these options, the picture is not saved in persistent storage, and the phone sends it again whenever the watchface restarts. When the plugin reports no image, the picture is removed from the watch.
- **Border:** configure the dial border and its weather-responsive behavior, including width, scheme, and shake-related options.
- **Seconds:** show or hide the second hand.

### Features

Tap a position in the layout preview to choose its content and appearance. The catalogue is grouped into categories:

- **Utilities:** battery, location, Pebble branding, Bluetooth, altitude, compass, and Quiet Time.
- **Health:** heart rate, daily steps, step-goal progress, sleep duration and quality, bedtime, and wake time. Availability depends on Pebble health data and permissions.
- **Date and time:** clock values, seconds, AM/PM, calendar dates, weekday/month labels, and week number.
- **Time zones:** fixed GMT offsets for selected cities and regions.
- **Weather:** current conditions and temperature, high/low, feels-like temperature, UV, rain chance, humidity, wind and direction, visibility, cloud cover, pressure, dew point, air quality, and forecast values at selected future offsets/days.
- **Astronomy:** moon phase, sunrise/sunset, visible planets and next rise, meteor shower, Saturn ring angle, next ISS pass, and aurora Kp index.
- **Wide combinations:** combined date, weather-update, health, and device-status readouts designed for wider positions.
- **Plugins:** up to twelve text values supplied by compatible phone-side plugin integrations.

Feature positions that do not fit the selected dial style are disabled unless the relevant compatibility override is enabled. The settings page also offers font, text-outline, icon-style, daily step-goal, and draw-order controls. Supported slots can have separate content for normal and shake states.

### Colors

Edit the main and accent colors and choose from the available color presets. An optional night scheme provides separate background, text, and accent colors. Presets can be filtered in the picker; the selected scheme is previewed in the settings page.

### Animation

Configure the startup clock animation, startup index animation, shake animation, shake-hand translucency, and shake animation duration.

### My Style Presets

Save up to six named snapshots of the Style, Colors, and Features configuration. Presets can be applied, renamed, deleted, exported as JSON, or imported from JSON. A preset can also be assigned a schedule: selected days and a local time. The phone must be connected at the scheduled time; if it is disconnected, the style is applied when it reconnects.

### Weather

Select the weather data source and units for temperature, wind speed, air quality, and altitude. Open-Meteo supplies cloud cover and forecast data; the Pebble app/plugin can provide selected current conditions, while OpenWeatherMap can optionally be configured with an API key as an additional source. The exact fields available depend on the selected source and its response.

### Location

Use phone GPS automatically, search for a place, or enter latitude and longitude manually. Location is used for weather and astronomical calculations.

### Updates

Set the data refresh interval, enable battery-saving behavior, and inspect service status. The watch may defer or reduce background work to conserve power.

### Other

Configure hourly vibration reminders, including interval, pattern, time window, active days, and whether they override Quiet Time. Bluetooth-loss vibration can also be enabled and configured.

### Debug

Developer tools include a custom test date/time, drawing diagnostics, font preview import, recent raw message inspection, editable raw data overrides, and a view of the current settings keyset. These controls are intended for development and troubleshooting.

## Build and development setup

The project targets Pebble Time 2 (`emery`) and uses the Pebble SDK native C toolchain together with the Pebble phone-side JavaScript runtime.

Install and configure:

- Pebble SDK and its compiler/toolchain for the `emery` target.
- Node.js for phone-side code and JavaScript generators.
- Python 3 for the procedural artwork generators.
- Python package `Pillow` for hand-icon rendering, plus `numpy` for the procedural weather-background renderers.
- Node packages used by image scripts: `sharp` and `pngjs`. The table editor itself has no third-party dependencies.

Install the optional generator dependencies from the project root (use a Python virtual environment where appropriate):

```sh
npm install --no-save sharp pngjs
python3 -m pip install Pillow numpy
```

The weather-icon preview generator needs `pngjs`; the font/preset preview processing and image optimizer need `sharp`. The Python hand-icon renderer needs Pillow. The procedural weather-background renderers need both Pillow and NumPy.

Some source assets and optional packages may not be present in a fresh checkout or archive. The generator scripts are developer utilities, not self-contained commands that can all run successfully without their input artwork and dependencies. Read the script-specific notes below before running an individual tool. `generate-all.js` reports failures and continues so missing optional inputs do not prevent unrelated generators from running; review its summary before building if you changed generated assets.

From the project root:

```sh
node scripts/generate-all.js
pebble build
```

Install to an attached watch or emulator using the usual Pebble CLI commands, for example:

```sh
pebble install --emulator emery
```

If you only changed C or ordinary JavaScript source and did not change generated inputs, you generally do not need to run every asset generator. Run the relevant generator when changing its source data, artwork, message-key list, or primitive IDs.

## Scripts reference

Run commands from the repository root unless noted otherwise. These scripts update generated files, `package.json`, or source artwork; review their output and commit the generated changes with the corresponding source change.

| Script | Purpose and inputs | Notes / prerequisites |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/generate-all.js` | Runs the build-time generation pipeline in dependency order, then optimizes embedded images. | Convenience wrapper; invokes the message-key generator, Python hand-icon renderer, preview generators, background generators, and image optimizer. It continues after individual failures and prints a summary. |
| `scripts/generate-message-keys.js` | Generates `src/c/generated/message_key_index.h` from the ordered `pebble.messageKeys` list in `package.json`. | Run after adding, removing, or reordering AppMessage keys. The generated indices must match the package list. |
| `scripts/generate-primitive-ids.js` | Generates the phone-side primitive ID lookup from `src/c/features/primitives/primitive_ids.h`. | Run after changing the C primitive enum or aliases. Do not edit the generated JavaScript lookup manually. |
| `scripts/check-scheduled-style-map.js` | Checks that scheduled-style field mappings remain aligned with the settings save payload and stored settings. | Validation only; run after adding or renaming a setting that should be included in scheduled styles. |
| `scripts/generate_hand_style_icons.py` | Renders small PNG silhouettes for the hand-style picker from the hand geometry definitions. | Python 3, Pillow, and NumPy required. Run before `generate-hand-style-icons.js` when hand geometry changes. |

| `scripts/generate-infographics.js` | Packages hand-style diagrams and marker-preset explainer images for settings popups. | Reads the relevant PNGs from `resources/infographics/`; outputs several generated preview modules. |

Node.js is required; the editor itself has no third-party dependencies and uses browser local storage for drafts. See `scripts/js table editor/readme.md`. |

### Generator workflow

The normal full pipeline is:

```sh
node scripts/generate-all.js
```

The wrapper runs the generators in the required order. In particular, the Python hand-style renderer runs before its JavaScript embedder, and `optimize-generated-images.js` runs last. The standalone Python weather-background renderers are not included in this pipeline: run them explicitly when you want to regenerate those source PNGs, then run `generate-weather-backgrounds.js` (or the full pipeline) to register and embed them.

Examples:

```sh
# Check scheduled-style mappings after changing settings
node scripts/check-scheduled-style-map.js

# Regenerate primitive IDs after editing the C enum
node scripts/generate-primitive-ids.js

# Render all procedural weather-background styles
python3 scripts/render-manhattan-weatherbg.py
python3 scripts/render-weatherbg-styles.py

# Preview image-size savings without modifying generated files
node scripts/optimize-generated-images.js --dry-run
```

## Working with settings and generated data

- `src/pkjs/config-page.js` assembles the settings page from the modules in `src/pkjs/config/`. The page is delivered as a bundled HTML payload through the phone/watch message channel; `config.html` is the small bootstrap page, not the complete settings UI.
- `src/pkjs/presets-lookups.js` is the hand-maintained source for fonts, color schemes, feature content categories, hand presets, and marker presets. Keep font IDs and resource names synchronized with `src/c/fonts/font_lookup.c` and `package.json`.
- `src/pkjs/settings/` contains settings defaults, codecs, and migrations. Watch-side defaults and data structures live in `src/c/data/`. When changing a setting, check both sides and the save/restore and scheduled-style mappings.
- `src/c/features/primitives/primitive_ids.h` is the canonical primitive ID list. Regenerate the phone-side ID lookup after changing it.
- Files under `src/c/generated/` and `src/pkjs/data/generated/` are generated outputs. Change their source inputs and run the appropriate generator instead of editing generated files directly.
- `package.json` defines the Pebble app, resources, capabilities, target platform, and ordered AppMessage keys. Regenerate the message-key header after changing that key list.

## Background and artwork conventions

- The watch display is 200 × 228 pixels. Keep watch bitmap assets compact and use the Pebble palette where practical.
- Numbered custom backgrounds live in `resources/backgrounds/` and are discovered by `generate-backgrounds.js`.
- Weather background styles live in `resources/weatherbg/<style>/`. Use the ten condition names `clear_sky`, `partly_cloudy`, `overcast`, `fog`, `drizzle`, `rain`, `heavy_rain`, `snow`, `thunderstorm`, and `sleet`, each with `_day.png` and `_night.png` variants where available.
- Settings-page preview artwork is embedded into generated JavaScript modules because the bundled settings page cannot fetch arbitrary local files at runtime. Regenerate the matching preview module whenever its source artwork changes.

## Architecture overview

The watch-side C application owns drawing and interaction. It receives compact settings and data messages, updates its in-memory watch model, persists configuration, and renders the dial, hands, markers, and feature slots. The rendering code includes bitmap and procedural marker treatments, custom hand geometry, subpixel drawing helpers, styled vector/bitmap icons, weather icon sets, forecast-offset icons, and color/contrast handling. Feature layout and primitive composition determine which readouts fit each dial position and how their elements are drawn relative to the hands. Separate timing and power modules manage time updates, feature refreshes, hourly vibration, and battery-saving behavior.

The phone-side JavaScript application owns configuration UI, external data retrieval, astronomical calculations, weather normalization, location handling, scheduling, settings codecs, and transfer of settings/data to the watch. The AppMessage schema in `src/pkjs/comms/message-schema.js`, the C decoder, and `package.json` message keys form a shared interface; changes to one side should be checked against the others.

## Data and rendering notes

The C feature renderer consumes primitive sequences rather than phone-side feature names. The phone settings catalogue maps user-facing content choices to primitive IDs and any associated values; the generated ID lookup keeps those values aligned with the C enum. When adding a new feature, update the phone-side catalogue/preview metadata and the corresponding C renderer, then regenerate primitive IDs if the enum changed. Check that each supported slot has suitable layout, color, and shake-state behavior.

Weather and astronomy are calculated or normalized on the phone before being sent to the watch. The watch renders the compact values it receives; it does not make network requests. Weather provider availability, location permissions, health permissions, phone connectivity, and background execution limits therefore affect which live values can be displayed. Missing or stale provider data is represented as unavailable rather than being calculated by the watch.

## Platforms and resources (emery + gabbro)

One repo builds both the Pebble Time 2 (emery, 200x228) and the Pebble Round 2 (gabbro, 260x260, no corner features).
`resources/` is split into `square/` (200x228 art), `round/` (260x260 art, same file names) and `common/` (fonts, small icons,
preview-only sources). `scripts/migrate-resources.js` moves an old layout over; `scripts/generate-all.js` runs it first.
See `round_support.md` for the full plan and status.

## Legacy black & white platforms (flint + diorite)

`flint` (Pebble 2 Duo) and `diorite` (Pebble 2) are 144x168, 1-bit and share one code path (`PBL_BW`). Plan and rationale: `legacy_support.md`.

- **Resources:** `resources/legacy/` holds the 1-bit art (`backgrounds`, `weatherbg`, `example-styles`, `indices`, `images/{simple,hollow,forecast_n}`, `images/icon.png`). Files are palettised 1-bit PNGs (`1BitPalette`). `common/*` entries in `package.json` are restricted to `emery, gabbro`; legacy entries target `flint, diorite`.
- **Stand-ins:** `python3 scripts/make-legacy-standins.py` derives missing legacy art from the square/common art (never overwrites; `--force`, `--only <group>`). Run it (or `scripts/generate-all.js`, which does) before the first flint/diorite build. Replace the stand-ins with hand-drawn art when available; legacy backgrounds/weather backgrounds are optional (the watch falls back to a plain background when none exist).
- **Look:** colours reduce to black/white (text is forced to contrast with the background), backgrounds are 2-level, the weather border is a pattern ring (conditions / rain chance), no full-colour icon style, no left/right edge slots, max 6 items per slot, smaller icons and pills, B&W fonts.
- **Phone side:** `platform.js` (`bw`, `bwByte`, `bwFont`), encoder/codecs reduce colours, the settings page hides unsupported options and previews in 1 bit.
- **Unverified (check on emulator/hardware):** 1-bit framebuffer bit order (`BW_FB_LSB_FIRST` in `platform_bw.h`), 12 px vector icons, font vertical offsets, `PRIMITIVE_FEATURE_MAX` of 6.
